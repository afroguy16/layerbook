## 2. How to fill it in

1. **Time:** seconds from the first tap after you finish reading the task, to the moment the task is done or the tester gives up.
2. **Taps:** read from the prototype's hidden tap counter (open the demo panel by long-pressing the AB logo), or count by hand.
3. **Errors:** count wrong taps that needed going back, plus wrong entries.
4. **Success:** **Y** = done alone; **H** = done with a hint; **N** = not done.
5. **Notes:** what they said (quote) and where they hesitated.

## 4. SUS (System Usability Scale)

Read each statement aloud; the tester answers 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Scoring: odd items = answer − 1; even items = 5 − answer; add up and multiply by 2.5 (gives 0–100). The average across \~500 studies is 68 ([MeasuringU](https://measuringu.com/sus/)). Reading it aloud and the plain wording below are our adaptations, untested, and may affect comparison with published averages.
