# Workshop visit script

*Context: the script Tega or a field marketer reads during an in-person workshop visit to test the clickable prototype; record results on the *[*Visit timing sheet*](https://ewvie.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/AB/pages/64225290/Visit+timing+sheet)*. Pidgin lines are our drafts and are ****untested****: check them with a native speaker before using them.*

## 1. What we're testing (memo)

1. **Logging adoption (the biggest risk).** Will mechanics log jobs in Auto Book instead of their exercise book? The [Idea brief](https://ewvie.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/AB/pages/62750721/Idea+brief+Auto+Book+family+doctor+for+your+car) lists "mechanics may not log jobs; many still use an exercise book" as a risk. **Target: car + issues + urgency logged in under a minute** (tasks 1–2).
2. **Notice channel.** Booking notices are going out by email and WhatsApp (decided 16 Oct 2026). Check which one owners actually read and which mechanics expect.
3. **Owners on WhatsApp.** Do owners understand the WhatsApp message and the car-record link, without an app or login (task 8)?
4. Also: the ₦500 / ₦200-with-code fee is clear (task 7); the agreed outside-fix reason chips are understood (task 4); issue and car-model chip lists are right (they are placeholders).

## 2. Before you go

1. Charge the test phone; open the prototype at [**https://autobk.pages.dev**](https://autobk.pages.dev) (it needs a data connection); set the copy toggle to English (switch to Pidgin only if the tester prefers).
2. Print or open a copy of the timing sheet; bring a stopwatch, or use the prototype's tap counter and timer: **on your own (host) phone, turn on Host tools in the settings drawer**. That shows the timer, tap count, reset and Next tester; **reset between testers with Host tools**. Leave Host tools off on tester phones; there is no logo long-press.
3. There is **one link / QR code**, the wizard: <https://autobk.pages.dev>.
4. Bring the "new car" card for task 2 (plate, owner name, phone, all dummy).
5. Plan 20–30 minutes per person. Don't visit at the busiest time; ask the Oga when is good.
6. Do **not** promise money, features or dates.

### QR code for testers

1. To let a tester open the prototype on their own phone, show or print the QR code for [**https://autobk.pages.dev**](https://autobk.pages.dev) (the image below). On your own (host) phone, turn on Host tools in the settings drawer to see the timer, and reset between testers there.
2. The QR code image is on this page, in this section (above). It scans to <https://autobk.pages.dev>. Scan it with a phone camera to check it opens the prototype before printing.
3. Print it at least 4 cm wide so it scans in a dim workshop.

## 3. Consent (read aloud, every time)

1. **English:** "Thank you for your time. I'm testing a simple phone app for workshops. I'm testing the app, not you, so there are no wrong answers. It takes about 20 minutes. I'll take notes and time how long things take. I won't record your voice or face, and I won't write your name in our notes. You can stop at any time. Is that OK?"
2. **Pidgin (untested):** "Thank you for your time. I dey test one small phone app for workshop. Na the app I dey test, no be you, so no wrong answer. E go take like 20 minutes. I go write small note and check how long tins take. I no go record your voice or face, I no go write your name. You fit stop anytime. E dey okay?"
3. Tick "Consent given" on the sheet only after a clear yes.

## 4. Warm-up questions (before showing the phone)

1. "How do customers usually book or arrange to bring their car? Walk-in, phone call, WhatsApp?" *(Pidgin, untested: "How customers dey book or arrange to bring dem motor? Dem just show, call, or WhatsApp?")*
2. "When you check a car, how do you keep a record of what you found? Can you show me?" (Ask to see the exercise book or paper, if they're happy to.) *("When you check motor, where you dey write wetin you see? You fit show me?")*
3. "When something needs fixing later, how do you remind the owner?" *("If tin go need fix later, how you dey remind the owner?")*
4. "Who in the workshop handles money and the phone with customers?" (Tells us who is Owner vs manager.)

## 5. Tasks (no explanation)

Hand over the phone. Say: **"Please do this the way you think it works. I can't help, but please say out loud what you're thinking."** *(Pidgin, untested: "Do am the way you think say e dey work. I no fit help you, but abeg talk wetin dey your mind as you dey do am.")*

Read each task, start the timer at the first tap, and don't help. If they're stuck for 60 seconds, say "Thank you, let's move on" and mark **N** (or give one hint and mark **H**).

1. "Chidi's Corolla is back. Add brakes, Fix now, and save."
2. "A new car came in \[hand the card\]. Add shocks, Fix now, and engine oil, Can wait."
3. "Mark the brake pads as done."
4. "Open Bisi's Camry, APP 789 EF. The owner fixed the wipers somewhere else. Show that in the app."
5. "Send Chidi his car record on WhatsApp."
6. "Book Chidi for tomorrow at 10am."
7. "Someone from our team gave you the code EARLY200. Use it, then tell me how much each booking costs."
8. **Owner task** (with a car owner, if one is present): open the WhatsApp mock. "You got this message. What does your car need? Book a time."
9. "Sign up as the Oga of this workshop, then find where the money is."

## 6. Think-aloud prompts (use sparingly)

1. "What are you looking for?" *("Wetin you dey find?")*
2. "What do you expect to happen if you tap that?" *("Wetin you think go happen if you press am?")*
3. "What does this word mean to you?" (point at "Fix soon" or "Marked outside Auto Book")
4. Never say "Tap there" or "That's wrong".

## 7. After the tasks

1. **SUS:** read the 10 statements on the timing sheet; the tester answers 1–5.
2. **Logging habit:** "Would you use this instead of your book for every car? What would stop you?" *("You go use am instead of your book for every motor? Wetin fit stop you?")*
3. **Notice channel:** "When a booking is made, how should the owner hear about it: WhatsApp, email, SMS, or a call? Which do your customers actually read?"
4. **Owner (WhatsApp vs web):** "Would you rather get everything as WhatsApp messages, or open a web page link? Why?"
5. **Fee:** "Each completed booking costs ₦500, or ₦200 with the code. What do you think of that?" (Just listen; don't sell.)
6. **Outside-fix chips:** "When you closed the wipers, were the three choices clear? Is any reason missing?"
7. **Chips:** "Which problems do you see most that weren't on the list? Which car models are missing?" (Write them down; the lists are placeholders.)
8. **Language:** "English or Pidgin on the buttons: which is easier?" (Show the toggle.)
9. "Anything else you'd change?"

## 8. Close

1. "Thank you, this really helps." Don't promise features or dates.
2. Straight after: fill in the "Top 3 problems" on the timing sheet while it's fresh.
